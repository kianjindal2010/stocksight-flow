How to Use StockSight Learner

A literacy-first workflow with Firebase auth and paper trading.

Core Backbone

  • Account/profile, workspace preferences, and notification settings are persistent per Firebase user.
  • Multi-market switching and benchmark context are available from the top market selector.
  • Search + quick quote is available from dashboard shortcuts and API tools.
  • Watchlists, alerts, portfolio, trade history, and trade journal are all learner-available.

Learner-First Features

  • Learning dashboard with progress and recommended next step.
  • Guided curriculum, concept checks, and challenge/milestone tracking.
  • Before-you-trade checklist is required before buy/sell execution.
  • Simplified insights: health score, explain-portfolio, and why-portfolio-moved.
  • Weekly learning loop: mistakes, good decisions, and next concept.
  • Risk Lab Lite and AI Forecast Lite with plain-English interpretation.

What Is Intentionally Not Included

  • Advanced quant dashboards and model diagnostics.
  • Derivatives, futures/options depth, and professional strategy tooling.
  • Team/admin/pro collaboration layers and advanced order complexity.

Reminder

This app is for education and practice only. It is not financial advice.
